# Training Impact on Teams
## Understanding the Impact of Cyber Security Training
* **Overview:** Explores the critical value of structured cybersecurity training and practice for both individual skill growth and organizational capacity building.
* **Key Benefits for Organisations:**
    * **Risk Reduction:** Practicing in safe training environments prevents costly mistakes on live production systems during a real incident.
    * **Increased Team Capacity:** Prepares teams in advance so they handle threats smoothly instead of learning under fire during an active cyber attack.
    * **Efficient Onboarding:** Makes it practical to hire junior staff by providing the means for them to ramp up quickly, saving senior staff from repeatedly teaching foundational material.
    * **Common Baseline:** Centralised training establishes clear skill baselines across teams, replacing vague titles with measurable insights for better task allocation and career progression.
    * **Culture & Teamwork:** Fosters camaraderie through collaborative challenges like Capture the Flag (CTF) events.

## Write a Cyber Security Training Investment Proposal
* **Overview:** Focuses on how to build a business case and mathematically prove the financial value of investing in employee cybersecurity training to secure budget approval from leadership.
* **Calculating Financial Impact & ROI:** Demonstrates how to quantify training gains using specific metrics:
    * **Total Team Cost:** Number of employees multiplied by their annual cost.
    * **Productivity Savings (Gains):** Total team cost multiplied by the estimated productivity increase percentage resulting from training (typically modelled around 4%).
    * **Total Training Cost:** Number of employees multiplied by the cost per training seat.
    * **Return on Investment (ROI):** Calculated by dividing total productivity savings by the total training cost, expressed as a percentage.
* **Proposal Best Practices:** Recommends utilizing structured templates and clear financial data to demonstrate that training functions as a revenue-protecting asset rather than an operational expense.

## Vendor Selection
* **Overview:** Focuses on the critical considerations and key questions organizations must evaluate when choosing the right cybersecurity training vendor.
* **Key Vendor Selection Questions:**
    * **Target Audience:** Who is the training intended for across the organization?
    * **Employee Profile:** What are the existing background, experience levels, and specific job roles of the staff?
    * **Vendor Track Record:** Has the vendor successfully worked with similar organizations or industry peers?
    * **Content Quality & Breadth:** Does the platform offer sufficient depth and quality across the specific technical topics required?
    * **Unified Platform:** Can users seamlessly learn, train, and practice their skills within a single platform?
    * **Cost vs. Value:** While cost matters to the CFO, the overall productivity gains and risk reduction typically far outweigh the initial training investment.
